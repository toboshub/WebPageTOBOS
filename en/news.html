Intellectual property in Colombia, explained plainly

Featured case · Patent litigation · March 13, 2026

Constitutional Court sets landmark precedent in a patent infringement case

In Colombia, patent disputes rarely reach the Constitutional Court, given the extremely high procedural technicality required for a tutela action (constitutional injunction) against judicial decisions.

After five years of complex litigation, Tobos Abogados SAS succeeded in elevating a technical patent infringement dispute to a matter of fundamental rights. The ruling corrected the course of the proceedings to enforce our client's rights before the Bogotá Superior Court and set a precedent for the constitutional protection of intellectual property and scientific innovation in the country.
