Founding Partner

Luz Andrea Tobos Mateus

Lawyer and Chemical Engineer

She leads the Intellectual Property practice at Tobos Abogados SAS. She holds a law degree from Universidad de los Andes, a specialization in Commercial Law from Universidad del Rosario and a degree in Chemical Engineering from Universidad de América. She has more than 26 years of experience in invention patents, industrial designs, utility models and trademarks.

She advises companies and professionals on patentability searches, drafting and prosecution of applications, freedom-to-operate opinions and litigation involving new creations. Before joining Tobos Abogados in 2008, she worked at Baker & McKenzie Colombia and at Cavelier Abogados.

She is a member of ASIPI and ACPI, and has taught at the graduate level and spoken at conferences in Colombia and the United States.
